## How I reviewed them

For every story sentence I checked three things: it lists at least one chunk ID, the chunk was actually retrieved in this run, and the exact quote it rests on appears in that chunk. The app does this in code first and then with two model calls. One rewrites each quote so it stands on its own, without seeing the claim. The other checks the claim against those rewritten quotes. Anything that fails is removed and logged with the reason. I then read the removals by hand and searched the full corpus for each one, to tell training-data content apart from the checks being too strict.

## Smaller issues I noticed reading the stories

- Within a stage of life the order is not always right. In the Douglass story the 1838 escape comes before the earlier failed escape attempt and Covey.
- Some sentences are thin. Douglass's "He was a father." is all the sources' "children" passage supported.
- One wording slip: Douglass's mother "was of a darker complexion than either **her** grandmother or grandfather". The source says "my" grandmother: the paraphrase swapped the pronoun, and the judge allowed it as a paraphrase.
- Franklin: "lived in London in 1726" comes from an image caption in Ford's book. It is in the corpus, but a caption is weak evidence.

| 28 | He once experienced a humorous incident on a train where the seat next to him was avoided by all passengers until a governor sat beside him, which then made the seat desirable to others. | d1ccc22-c0515 | “Among the passengers on this train was Gov. George N. Briggs. I was not acquainted with him, and had no idea that I was known to him, however, I was, for upon observing me, the governor left his place, and making his way toward me, respectfully asked the privilege of a seat by my side; and upon introducing himself, we entered into a conversation very pleasant and instructive to me. The despised seat now became honored. His excellency had removed all the prejudice against sitting by the side of a Negro; and upon his leaving it, as he did, on reaching Pittsfield, there were at least one dozen applicants for the place.” | verified |
