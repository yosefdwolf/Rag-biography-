Each question is retrieved on its own. The model sees only the chunks listed, and says not in the sources when they don't answer it.

Every claim, in each answer and in the story, has to quote a retrieved chunk word for word. Code checks the quote really is in that chunk and that every number and name in the claim appears there, then a separate model call checks the quote actually says what the claim says. Claims that fail are removed. They are what the model tried to add from its own training data.
